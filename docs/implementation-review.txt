SolarQuote 1.1.0 — implementation review
Date: 2026-10-07

Implemented locally

Automatic equipment selection:
- Inverter tiers: 1, 3, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, 16, 18, 20, 25, 30, 40, 50 kW;
  supported parallel 50 kW configurations provide up to 500 kW nominal AC capacity,
  subject to the demand/headroom, PV and battery limits.
- Compatible 24 V, 48 V and high-voltage battery classes, actual nominal bank
  voltage, module topology, voltage envelope and current limits are selected
  together. Battery capacity follows the actual edited hourly night profile.
- PV DC capacity is independent of inverter AC capacity. Unsupported demand
  blocks a completed quote instead of substituting undersized equipment.
- Optional measured simultaneous and starting demand, and no-storage operation.
- Default 120 kWh/month with storage: 1 kW estimated planning assembly,
  1.3 kWp PV, 2.56 kWh battery, 24 V class / 25.6 V nominal. The 1 kW assembly
  requires storage, an external BMS and an approved grid transfer arrangement;
  it cannot export to the grid. The compatible small batteryless choice is 3 kW.
- Catalog sources, review date, three-phase and manufacturer compatibility
  limitations are included in the app and proposals. These are planning designs.

All 25 audited defects addressed in local source:
1. Hourly profiles and night-energy summaries share one authoritative profile.
2. PV and inverter losses both affect production using a disclosed DC/AC convention.
3. PV conversion, charging, discharge, inverter and export limits constrain dispatch.
4. Catalog overflows return a design error; no undersized clamp.
5. Billing reconnect/restore/resume paths call Play appropriately.
6. Purchase launch runs on Android's UI thread and surfaces its immediate result.
7. PDF embeds licensed Unicode fonts; real fontTools replaces the previous stubs.
8. Every export includes engineering warnings and avoids unsupported matched claims.
9. Daily and annual consumption share monthly x 12 / 365.
10. Live invalid/nonfinite financial and energy inputs are rejected before calculation.
11. Steady-state SOC uses a bounded fixed-point solver; failure is explicit.
12. Pending purchase deltas preserve existing paid access.
13. Trial eligibility, renewal terms and annual savings use actual Play offer phases.
14. All relevant purchases are acknowledged; failures are persisted and retried.
15. Bounded offline/migration access, signed server verification and RTDN code.
    Server mode remains inactive until credentials, endpoint and public key exist.
16. INTERNET permission and validated portable TLS CA bundle configured.
17. Verified time advances with elapsed time and retries; quote dates use UTC+8.
18. Dirty-draft protection, current-edit duplication and durable incomplete drafts.
19. Current-client report preview refreshes on entry.
20. Every PDF page receives the free watermark, including automatic continuations.
21. Reports use immutable snapshots, scheduled GPU capture, workers and atomic files.
22. CI collects matching unstripped symbols and retains the ABI directory.
23. CI strictly verifies the expected release signer and rejects unsigned entries.
24. Spreadsheet and other exports include capacities, assumptions and version/quote ID.
25. Packaged report assets resolve independently of the process working directory.

Additional recommendations implemented:

  cache, geometry-change invalidation and all-sky weather calibration.
- Panel/battery degradation, quoted replacements, discount rate, NPV, discounted
  payback and cost/tariff/yield sensitivity. Aging and yield effects are disclosed
  approximations based on first-year displacement, not full lifetime hourly dispatch.
- Chart legends, units, stored-energy meaning and grid sign convention.
- Report history, app-copy deletion, user-selected document destinations and
  Android document-URI backup import. Backup excludes subscription settings/tokens.
- Actual live Android system-bar/cutout insets, keyboard handling and back navigation.
- Responsive headings, fields, financial summaries and equipment information.
- Pinned build toolchain/dependencies, pure source fontTools recipe, portable
  chardet dependency and recursive ELF/16 KB checks across Python bundles.
- Fast Python and Supabase Deno CI before packaging; local privacy copy updated.
